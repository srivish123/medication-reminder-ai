📱 Medication Reminder & Information App

An AI-powered medication management application designed to help users take medicines on time, understand drug usage, detect harmful drug interactions, and improve medication adherence.
🚀 Problem Statement

Many people forget to take medications or lack awareness about drug interactions and side effects. This can lead to serious health risks. Existing reminder apps rarely provide intelligent safety checks.
Our solution addresses this gap using AI-driven drug interaction analysis combined with smart reminders.
