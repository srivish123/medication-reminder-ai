#**📱 Medication Reminder & Information App**

An AI-powered medication management application designed to help users take medicines on time, understand drug usage, detect harmful drug interactions, and improve medication adherence.

###**🚀 Problem Statement**

Many people forget to take medications or lack awareness about drug interactions and side effects. This can lead to serious health risks. Existing reminder apps rarely provide intelligent safety checks.
Our solution addresses this gap using __AI-driven drug interaction analysis combined with smart reminders.__

###**💡 Solution Overview**

The Medication Reminder & Information App provides:

+Timely medication reminders

+Verified drug information and side effects

+AI-based drug interaction checks

+Medication adherence tracking

This application focuses on practical healthcare safety, ease of use, and responsible AI integration.

###**✨ Key Features**

⏰ Smart medication reminders

📖 Drug information and side effects

🤖 AI-powered drug interaction checker

📊 Medication adherence tracking

🧭 Simple and accessible user interface
